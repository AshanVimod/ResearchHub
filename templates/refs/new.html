Tips

  • Titles are converted to sentence case for IEEE. Acronyms like LSTM stay as they are. Check proper nouns before saving.
  • arXiv only gives full names, so the last word becomes the family name. Fix names like “Van der Berg” by hand.
  • Cite keys follow family + year + first word, e.g. lecun2015deep.
  • Have a reference manager export? Import BibTeX.
